Connect with Instagram
What Tyve will access
Profile info (instagram_business_basic): your account ID and username, to show which account is connected and route messages to the right business.
Direct messages (instagram_business_manage_messages): receive the DMs customers send to your account and send the assistant's replies in the same conversation.
Tyve does not post, comment, or read your media, followers or insights.

You can disconnect at any time in Instagram Settings > Apps and websites. Read our Privacy Policy and data deletion instructions.
