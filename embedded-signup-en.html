Before you start

Tap each item below to confirm. Once all three are checked, the connect button becomes available.

  • ✓
    You use WhatsApp Business
    Your number is on the WhatsApp Business app (the dark green icon), not regular WhatsApp. If you still use the regular app, download WhatsApp Business and move your number over before continuing.
  • ✓
    Your app is up to date
    Open your app store (Play Store or App Store) and make sure WhatsApp Business is on the latest version.
  • ✓
    You have your phone with you
    During the connection you'll confirm on your phone (via a notification or QR code). Keep it nearby.

Connect now

When you tap the button, an official window from Meta (WhatsApp's owner) will open. Log in with your account, pick your number, and authorize. It takes about 2 minutes.

How it works: the Meta window asks for your login and shows your number for you to authorize. Then WhatsApp on your phone asks for a quick confirmation (notification or QR code). That's it: the automated assistant starts working alongside your WhatsApp — you keep sending and receiving messages normally on your phone.

WhatsApp connected successfully!

Your number is now linked to Tyve's automated assistant. Keep using WhatsApp on your phone as usual.

What's next? Our team will finish the setup and let you know as soon as your assistant is live — usually within a few hours. There's nothing else you need to do here. Any questions, reach us on WhatsApp.
